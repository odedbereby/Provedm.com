# Provedm

> Official product overview for Provedm, an iPhone app for seeing goals, calendars, reminders, and notes in one clear view.

Provedm combines every calendar available in Apple Calendar, including Google and Outlook calendars, with reminders and quick notes. It shows what is happening now, what is next, and what is due without opening the app.

## Widgets

- **Today at a Glance (Home Screen):** Shows reminders due today, today's events with the event in progress marked, and tomorrow's first event. Its calendar page moves forward as events finish and remains on the last page at the end of the day.
- **Today, Inbox, and Next Reminder (Lock Screen):** Shows upcoming events and the remaining event count for the week, open reminders in priority order, and the next reminder alert within the next 24 hours.
- **New Note:** A Control Center and Lock Screen control for quickly capturing a note.

## Calendar support

Provedm reads Apple Calendar. To include Google or Outlook, add the account in iPhone Settings: **Apps > Calendar > Calendar Accounts > Add Account**. Choose Google or Microsoft Exchange / Outlook.com, sign in, and enable Calendars. Then select the calendars to show in Provedm's Settings.

## Inside the app

- **Agenda:** The coming seven days, grouped by day, with a live marker for the event in progress.
- **Reminders:** Overdue and due-today reminders first. Reminders can be completed, edited, or deleted in place; repeating reminders continue after the current occurrence is completed.
- **Notes:** Quick notes can be created from the app or the New Note control.

## System requirements

Provedm is for iPhone and requires iOS 18 or later.
