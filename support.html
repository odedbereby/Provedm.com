Support

Provedm for iPhone · iOS 18 or later

Need help or found a problem? Email provedm.support@gmail.com. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version (Settings > General > About) and what you expected to happen.

Common questions

How do I see my Google or Outlook calendar in Provedm?

Provedm reads Apple Calendar, so add the account there: Settings > Apps > Calendar > Calendar Accounts > Add Account, choose Google or Microsoft Exchange / Outlook.com, sign in and switch Calendars on. Then open Provedm's Settings (the gear) and make sure those calendars are switched on.

How do I add the widgets?

Home screen: touch and hold an empty spot, tap +, search for Provedm and choose Today at a Glance. Lock screen: touch and hold the Lock Screen, tap Customize, then add Today, Inbox or Next Reminder. For quick notes, add the New Note control in Control Center or on the lock screen. The Welcome Guide in Provedm's Settings walks through this too.

The home widget changed page on its own.

That is intended. Today at a Glance shows the page of your calendar holding the next event that has not ended, moves on when it finishes, and rests on the last page at the end of the day. If you turn a page by hand it holds for a few seconds, then follows the day again.

Why does Provedm ask for Calendar and Reminders access?

To show your events and reminders. Each is optional and requested only when you open the matching tab or use the matching widget. Your data is read on your iPhone and never leaves it. If you tapped "Don't Allow", turn access back on in Settings > Provedm.

The Agenda or Reminders tab says "Connect Apple Calendar" or "Connect Apple Reminders".

Access for that app is off. Tap Allow Access or Open Settings, then switch it on. Notes always work without any access.

A widget says "Tap to allow Calendar" or "Tap to allow Reminders".

Widgets cannot ask for permission themselves. Tap the widget to open Provedm, which asks for the access. After you allow it, the widget fills in within a few minutes.

My widgets are out of date.

iOS decides how often widgets refresh. Provedm asks for an update every few minutes and whenever you change something in the app. If widgets lag, open Provedm once, and check that Background App Refresh is on (Settings > General > Background App Refresh > Provedm). Low Power Mode also delays updates.

The Agenda is missing events.

Provedm shows the next seven days and hides events that already ended. Open Provedm's Settings (the gear) and make sure the calendar is switched on in the list. Apple does not share the Calendar app's own selection with other apps, so choose your calendars there once.

What happens when I complete a repeating reminder?

Only the current occurrence is completed and the series continues. To delete the whole series, swipe left and choose Series.

Where are my notes stored? Can I move them to a new iPhone?

Notes are stored only on your iPhone, inside Provedm. They are not synced. They can be included in your iPhone's backup, so restoring a backup to a new iPhone brings them along.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings > Account > Delete Account in Provedm. Your account, linked logins and sessions are removed from our servers right away. Provedm works without an account, and notes, events and reminders never leave your iPhone.

I can't sign in, or I see two accounts

Sign in with the same Apple or Google login you used before. To use both logins on one account, sign in with one, then open Settings > Account > Sign-in methods and link the other. A login already used by a different Provedm account cannot be linked; delete that account first if you no longer need it. Still stuck? Email provedm.support@gmail.com.

How do I delete my on-device data?

Delete a note by swiping it left in the Notes tab. Deleting the app removes all notes and preferences. Events and reminders belong to Apple Calendar and Apple Reminders, so delete those in the Apple apps.
