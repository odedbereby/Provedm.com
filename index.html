The main event

Widgets that show what matters next.

Glance at your phone and know your day. Complete a reminder or flip a page right from the widget.

  • Today at a GlanceHome screen. Reminders due today, today's events with the one in progress marked, and tomorrow's first event. The calendar turns its own page as events finish and rests on the last page by the end of the day.
  • Today, Inbox and Next ReminderLock screen. Your next events and how many more the week holds, open reminders in the order that matters, and the next reminder alert in the coming 24 hours.
  • New NoteA Control Center and lock screen control, so a thought becomes a note before you forget it.

Google & Outlook

Bring your other calendars along.

Provedm reads Apple Calendar, so add your accounts there once and they appear in the app and in every widget.

Open Calendar AccountsSettings › Apps › Calendar › Calendar Accounts.
Add AccountChoose Google, or Microsoft Exchange / Outlook.com, and sign in. Switch Calendars on.
Pick them in ProvedmOpen Provedm's Settings (the gear) and choose which calendars to show.
