Provedm

Calendar, reminders and notes in one calm place.

Provedm shows your next seven days from Apple Calendar, your open reminders from Apple Reminders, and a private notes list, with widgets for your lock screen and home screen. Everything stays on your iPhone.

Private by design. No account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Provedm never sends your calendar, reminders or notes anywhere.
  • Agenda. The next seven days, grouped by day, with a live marker for what is happening now.
  • Reminders. Overdue and due-today first. Complete, edit or delete in place; repeating reminders keep going.
  • Notes. Quick private notes, stored only on your device.
  • Widgets. Today, Inbox and Next Reminder for the lock screen, and Today at a Glance for the home screen.
  • You choose the access. Notes work without any permission. Calendar and Reminders are requested only when you use them, and each is optional.
