Provedm

All your calendars, one clear view. Widgets that show what matters next.

Provedm merges every calendar in Apple Calendar, including Google and Outlook, with your reminders and quick notes, then presents them so you can see what is now, what is next and what is due without opening anything.

Widgets are the main event

  • Today at a Glance (home screen). Reminders due today, one at a time, today's events with the one in progress marked, and tomorrow's first event. The calendar turns its own page as events finish and rests on the last page by the end of the day. Complete a reminder or flip pages right from the widget.
  • Today (lock screen). Your next events and how many more the week holds.
  • Inbox (lock screen). Open reminders in the order that matters: due today first, then undated, then the next three days.
  • Next Reminder (lock screen). The next reminder alert in the coming 24 hours.
  • New Note (Control Center and lock screen). Capture a note the moment you think of it.

To add a widget, touch and hold the Home Screen, tap +, and search for Provedm. Lock screen widgets are added by touching and holding the Lock Screen.

Bring Google and Outlook along

Provedm reads Apple Calendar, so add your other calendars there once and they appear in the app and in every widget: open Settings > Apps > Calendar > Calendar Accounts > Add Account, choose Google or Microsoft Exchange / Outlook.com, sign in and switch Calendars on. Then pick the calendars to show in Provedm's Settings.

Inside the app

  • Agenda. The next seven days from all your calendars, grouped by day, with a live marker for what is happening now.
  • Reminders. Overdue and due-today first. Complete, edit or delete in place; repeating reminders keep going.
  • Notes. Quick notes, one tap away from the app or the New Note control.
  • Quick capture. Add an event, reminder or note in seconds.
  • You choose the access. Notes work without any permission. Calendar and Reminders are requested only when you use them, and each is optional.
  • Welcome Guide. A short walkthrough of all this, any time, in Settings.
